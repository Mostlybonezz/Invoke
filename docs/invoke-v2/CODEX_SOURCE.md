# Appendix A — Complete Life Maintenance Codex content brief

This is source coverage for implementation. The cadence labels are configurable organizing suggestions unless an actual manufacturer, professional, legal or verified safety requirement supplies the interval. Split or combine adjacent actions sensibly; preserve every action in the coverage map. Specialized work must be assigned to the responsible person/professional, not automatically to the user.

**Key:** D daily/after use; W weekly; M monthly; Q quarterly; S semiannual/seasonal; Y annual; C condition-based; E event-based; P professional/manufacturer/personalized schedule. A range such as M/Q means choose an appropriate review cadence during setup, not duplicate both.

## C01 — Physical health and biological maintenance → Vitality

1. [D/P] Oral care: brushing and interdental care according to appropriate guidance.
2. [D/C] Personal/body hygiene appropriate to activity and needs.
3. [D/W] Sleep opportunity, preferred schedule and review of sleep quality.
4. [D/W] Food, fluids and nutritional adequacy; coordinate with meal planning rather than duplicate it.
5. [D/P] Prescribed medication adherence; record only the user's established instructions.
6. [D/W] Ordinary movement and breaking up unwanted prolonged inactivity.
7. [W/P] Planned strength training and aerobic activity, individually configured.
8. [W/C] Recovery, fatigue, discomfort, injury and exercise-performance review.
9. [W] Meal/grocery planning links to C06.
10. [M/Q, optional] User-chosen fitness, body or other relevant health measurements without diagnosis.
11. [C/P] Persistent pain, unexplained symptoms or significant changes: arrange appropriate care rather than wait for an annual review.
12. [M/P] Medication supply, refill lead time and access to established prescriptions.
13. [Q/P] Review nutrition, activity, sleep and energy patterns.
14. [P] Preventive appointments, risk/age-appropriate screening, vaccinations, dental care and indicated vision/hearing review.
15. [P/E] Discuss medication effectiveness/side effects with the relevant clinician; do not make automated dose changes.
16. [Y/E] Maintain medication, allergy, personal medical and relevant family-history records.

## C02 — Grooming and physical presentation → Regalia / Vitality

1. [D/W] Hair cleanliness, styling, facial/body hair and preferred grooming.
2. [D/P] Skin care and sun protection appropriate to the person's needs and product instructions.
3. [W/C] Nail hygiene, trimming and attention to changes.
4. [W/C] Clean combs, brushes, razors, trimmers and other tools using their instructions.
5. [W/P] Maintain cosmetic applicators/brushes where applicable.
6. [M/C] Haircut or trim based on desired style; no universal compulsory interval.
7. [M/Q] Inspect grooming equipment, blades, charging accessories and supplies.
8. [M/C] Replenish personal-care products; inspect labeled expiry/after-opening guidance and deterioration.
9. [Q/S] Review desired hairstyle, facial hair, skincare and overall presentation.
10. [Y/C] Simplify routines, remove unused products and replace damaged equipment.
11. [C] Store fragrance and sensitive products appropriately; investigate changes in condition.

## C03 — Clothing, wardrobe and style → Regalia

1. [D/after wear] Sort laundering needs by garment, use and care label; treat stains promptly.
2. [D/after wear] Air or store suitable rewearable clothing; allow footwear to dry.
3. [W] Wash, dry, fold/hang and put away laundry with garment-appropriate care.
4. [W/C] Clean frequently used footwear; inspect obvious damage.
5. [W/C] Repair loose buttons, stitching and small tears; flag tailoring/zipper work.
6. [M] Inspect socks, underwear and base layers for fit, thinning, holes and elastic failure.
7. [M] Inspect shirts, pants/jeans, activewear and sleepwear for wear, stains and loss of shape.
8. [M/C] Inspect sweaters/knitwear for pilling or damage; de-pill and store appropriately.
9. [M/C] Check shoe soles, traction, structure, insoles and laces; maintain suitable materials.
10. [M/C] Clean/inspect jewelry, watches, belts, wallets, bags and accessories.
11. [M/Q] Identify actual missing essentials and planned replacements.
12. [Q] Try on/review fit; retain, repair, tailor, sell, donate or retire deliberately.
13. [Q] Review outfit combinations, underused items, desired silhouette/colors/textures and style experiments.
14. [S] Transition seasonal clothing; clean and protect items before storage.
15. [S] Inspect coats, jackets and boots, including material care, weather protection, lining and hardware.
16. [S/Y] Update a style reference board and review what is actually worn.
17. [Y] Inventory worthwhile wardrobe records and estimate condition-based future costs.
18. [Y] Review durable purchases and evolving style without requiring replacement of serviceable clothing.

## C04 — Sleep environment and bedroom → Hearth / Vitality

1. [D/W] Straighten bedding and keep the sleeping area usable; making the bed is optional.
2. [D/W] Maintain comfortable light, temperature and airflow.
3. [W/P] Launder pillowcases and sheets on an appropriate configured schedule.
4. [W] Dust/vacuum and reduce obstructive clutter.
5. [M/Q/P] Clean blankets, duvet covers, comforters and mattress protectors per use/care labels.
6. [M/Q] Clean below/behind furniture and organize drawers/closets.
7. [M/Q/C] Inspect pillows, bedding and mattress cleanliness, support and condition.
8. [M/Q] Check excess moisture, odors and signs of mold; address causes.
9. [Q/C] Clean curtains/blinds, windows and tracks as appropriate.
10. [P/C] Rotate a mattress only if its manufacturer recommends it.
11. [Y/C] Repair/replace damaged furnishings/bedding or inadequate support; adjust room layout when useful.

## C05 — Bathroom and hygiene facilities → Hearth

1. [W/C] Clean toilet, basin, mirror, shower/tub and frequently touched surfaces.
2. [W/C] Launder bath/hand towels and bath mats based on use and drying conditions.
3. [D/W] Replenish toilet paper, soap and other essentials.
4. [W/C] Clean reusable holders, dishes and dispensers.
5. [M/Q/C] Remove drain hair/debris and mineral buildup using fixture-appropriate methods.
6. [M/Q/P] Clean accessible ventilation components safely.
7. [M/Q] Inspect toilet operation, water connections, fixtures and leaks.
8. [M/Q] Inspect grout, caulking and sealant; flag deterioration.
9. [M/Q/C] Check moisture damage, persistent odors and mold; report or arrange repair.
10. [M/Q] Review product condition/expiry and inspect reusable cleaning tools.
11. [Y/C] Repair fixtures or refer to the responsible party; replace liners/tools when cleaning no longer restores suitable condition.

## C06 — Food, kitchen and nutrition infrastructure → Hearth / Vitality

1. [D] Clean dishes, cookware, utensils and preparation surfaces.
2. [D/P] Store perishables/leftovers safely, label relevant dates and manage food waste.
3. [D/W] Maintain access to suitable meals; plan purchases and staple quantities.
4. [W] Review leftovers/perishables and grocery needs without treating every quality date as an automatic safety expiry.
5. [W] Clean sinks, stovetop, microwave and frequently used appliances.
6. [W/C] Wash kitchen towels/cloths and maintain or replace cleaning sponges/tools as appropriate.
7. [M/Q] Inspect pantry goods for spoilage, pests and storage problems.
8. [M/Q] Clean refrigerator interiors/seals and check relevant temperature settings/measurements.
9. [M/Q/P] Clean oven, range hood and related components when needed.
10. [M/Q/C] Maintain knives/tools; inspect cutting boards and cookware for damage or loss of cleanability.
11. [D/W/C] Clean reusable bottles, seals and other frequently used food-contact containers.
12. [P] Replace water filters by specified time/capacity; follow appliance care instructions.
13. [M/Q] Review food spending, waste, meal suitability and cooking workflow.
14. [Y/C] Review appliance repair/replacement needs and reorganize storage.

## C07 — General household cleaning and organization → Hearth

1. [D/W] Remove trash/recycling and maintain clear common areas.
2. [D/W] Clean high-use surfaces and put possessions/tools in designated storage.
3. [W/C] Vacuum, sweep, mop and dust appropriate surfaces.
4. [M/C] Clean windows, mirrors, doors, trim and baseboards.
5. [M/C] Vacuum upholstery, beneath furniture and difficult-to-reach areas.
6. [M/Q] Clean frequently used cupboards/storage and address developing clutter.
7. [M/C] Check furniture fasteners, hinges, drawers, finishes and other repair needs.
8. [Q/S] Deep-clean selected rooms, rugs/carpets and neglected areas using suitable methods.
9. [Q/S] Review drawers, closets, garage/utility storage and rarely used possessions.
10. [Q/S] Keep, repair, sell, donate, recycle or dispose; maintain tools/spares/cleaning supplies.
11. [Q/C] Inspect for pests, leaks, odors or dampness and address the cause.
12. [Y/E] Update significant-possession inventory and useful storage/furniture arrangements.

## C08 — Home systems, utilities and property → Hearth / Stewardship

1. [P] Test smoke/CO alarms and track labeled end-of-service requirements.
2. [P] Inspect fire extinguishers and arrange service/replacement according to their instructions.
3. [M/P] Inspect heating/cooling/air-purifier filters and maintain ventilation equipment.
4. [D/after use] Clean dryer lint screen as instructed; arrange appropriate exhaust inspection/cleaning separately.
5. [M/C] Check visible plumbing and appliance connections for leaks.
6. [M/C] Observe outlets, switches, cords and plugs for damage, heat or abnormal operation; refer electrical faults appropriately.
7. [M/Q] Check doors, windows, locks, seals and weatherstripping.
8. [M/Q] Review unusual utility-use changes without claiming an automatic diagnosis.
9. [S/P] Prepare/service heating/cooling and inspect relevant water-heater maintenance needs.
10. [S/C] Inspect responsible areas of drainage, gutters, roof, siding, garage doors, gates and outdoor structures; no hazardous DIY instructions.
11. [E] Check for storm-related water intrusion or other damage.
12. [Y/C] Maintain a repair-priority/budget list; coordinate landlord/owner/qualified professional responsibilities.

## C09 — Vehicle and transportation → Wayfaring

1. [M/P] Check tire pressures, including a relevant spare, according to vehicle/tire guidance.
2. [M/C] Inspect tread, abnormal wear, cracks, bulges or other damage.
3. [M/C] Check exterior lights/signals, wipers and washer supply.
4. [W/M/P] Monitor dashboard warnings, oil/fluid checks and sufficient fuel/charge.
5. [W/M] Clean interior, mats, windows and exterior as needed.
6. [P] Follow the actual vehicle's time/mileage maintenance plan for oil, filters, fluids and other service.
7. [P/C] Tire rotation/alignment, brakes, battery and unusual handling/noises require appropriate inspection/service.
8. [Q/E] Check applicable manufacturer recalls using an authoritative source.
9. [E] Record repairs, services, mileage and costs; attach records where supported.
10. [Y/E] Renew registration, insurance and relevant roadside coverage by actual dates.
11. [S/Y] Inspect vehicle emergency supplies.
12. [Y] Review transportation costs, suitability and upcoming service/replacement budgets.

## C10 — Finances and resource management → Stewardship

1. [W] Review balances, upcoming bills, expected deposits and transactions.
2. [W/C] Investigate errors/unrecognized charges; do not create automated bank actions.
3. [W/M] Track spending relative to user-chosen plans.
4. [M] Pay obligations and verify automatic payments; deadlines remain explicit.
5. [M] Record planned savings and emergency reserves.
6. [M] Review debt balances, rates and user-selected payoff progress.
7. [M] Audit subscriptions/memberships and expected irregular expenses.
8. [M] Plan replacement reserves and major purchases using user-entered amounts.
9. [Q] Compare recurring service/insurance costs and review financial direction.
10. [Q/Y] Review savings/investment/retirement goals and employee benefits without prescribing investments.
11. [Y/E] Organize tax information and applicable filing/payment deadlines.
12. [Y/E] Review insurance, credit records, major assets/liabilities and changed financial needs.

## C11 — Administration, identity and legal records → Stewardship

1. [M/Q] Review renewal/expiry/contract deadlines and important correspondence.
2. [M/Q] File significant receipts, statements, ownership and service documents.
3. [M/Q] Maintain secure records and appropriate backups/access arrangements.
4. [Y/E] Check licenses, identification and passports against actual dates/travel needs.
5. [Y/E] Maintain birth/identity, employment, tax, insurance and relevant medical documents.
6. [Y/E] Review leases, titles/deeds, vehicle records, warranties and contractual obligations.
7. [E] Update address/contact information after changes.
8. [Y/E/P] Review beneficiaries, healthcare directives, wills/powers of attorney where relevant, with appropriate professional support.
9. [E] Maintain voter registration and respond to required civic notices where applicable.
10. [Y/E] Ensure essential documents are accessible to authorized people in contingencies.

## C12 — Technology, privacy and digital life → Artifice

1. [W/P] Apply supported software/security updates to relevant devices, routers and applications.
2. [W/P] Maintain important-file backups and adequate usable storage.
3. [W/M] Organize incoming documents, photos, media and important messages.
4. [M/C] Check performance, unusual behavior and device condition.
5. [M/P] Clean screens, input devices, peripherals and ventilation/dust filters safely.
6. [M/C] Inspect cables, chargers, connectors and batteries; damaged power accessories need attention.
7. [M/Q] Review installed apps, unused software and subscriptions.
8. [Q] Test restoring representative important backups.
9. [Q] Review unique credentials/passkeys, multifactor authentication and recovery options.
10. [Q] Audit permissions, connected accounts/devices and obsolete accounts.
11. [Q/Y] Review security-support status, archive strategy and unnecessary data retention.
12. [Y/C] Plan hardware/battery replacement or upgrades based on support, condition and needs.
13. [Y/E] Maintain useful purchase/warranty/hardware records and overall privacy preferences.

## C13 — Employment and professional development → Craft

1. [W] Maintain work schedule, obligations and necessary clothing/equipment.
2. [W/M] Review immediate priorities and keep relevant skills active.
3. [M] Verify pay, hours and payroll records.
4. [M] Record achievements, responsibilities and useful feedback.
5. [M/Q] Review workload, working conditions, satisfaction and opportunities for efficiency.
6. [Q] Consider compensation, opportunities, career direction and useful training.
7. [Q/P] Complete selected professional learning and required training.
8. [Y/E] Update résumé/profiles and employment records.
9. [Y/P] Review benefits/enrollment dates, licenses and certifications.
10. [Y] Assess how work supports longer-term goals.

## C14 — Friendship, social connection and network development → Bonds

1. [W/flexible] Make room for meaningful contact with chosen existing friends.
2. [W/flexible] Respond to messages and follow up on relevant events as desired.
3. [W/M] Participate in shared conversations/activities; initiate specific plans.
4. [M] Reconnect with someone the user wants to remain close to.
5. [M/Q] Review whether desired contact and enjoyment are being met.
6. [Q] Consider reconnection, availability, upcoming occasions and contact preferences.
7. [W/growth mode] Attend a recurring social setting suited to the user's interests.
8. [W/growth mode] Initiate conversations and practice listening/conversational skills.
9. [W/M] Follow up with promising acquaintances and invite someone to a specific activity.
10. [M] Explore a new community/activity and review which settings support enjoyable repeat contact.
11. [Q] Review opportunities and barriers such as schedules, transport or limited access.
12. [Q] Adjust chosen expansion intensity; presets are editable heuristics, not scientific quotas.
13. [E/C] Address constructive conflict, maintain boundaries and observe reciprocity without automated scoring.

## C15 — Family, romantic and household relationships → Bonds

1. [W/flexible] Maintain desired family contact and time for relationships/dating as relevant.
2. [W] Communicate shared-living needs and resolve practical logistics.
3. [M] Arrange meaningful shared experiences where desired.
4. [M/Q] Review boundaries, commitments and expectations.
5. [M/Q] Review dating/partnership intentions without an imposed moral target.
6. [M/E] Coordinate voluntarily held family/dependent-care responsibilities affecting schedules or finances.
7. [Q/E] Reassess household arrangements, allocation of responsibilities and changes in needs.

## C16 — Spirituality, belief formation and magical practice → Arcana

1. [D/flexible] Chosen meditation, contemplation, prayer, visualization or ritual; duration is user-selected.
2. [D/C] Record meaningful dreams, intuitions, symbols, experiences and questions.
3. [D/flexible] Set an intention and connect applicable outcomes with concrete actions.
4. [D/W] Observe how thoughts, emotions and perception relate to decisions without unsupported causal claims.
5. [W] Study a spiritual/philosophical concept using a primary source or carefully sourced account.
6. [W] Practice/develop a chosen technique and record origin/method.
7. [W] Examine unfamiliar worldviews without requiring immediate agreement.
8. [W] Record contradictions, interpretations, alternative explanations and unanswered questions.
9. [W/M] Revisit practice notes and earlier observations.
10. [M] Select a focused practice; optionally undertake an extended ritual/reflective session.
11. [M/E] Observe user-chosen lunar/seasonal/astrological occasions without fabricated computed dates or predictions.
12. [M] Compare previous expectations with actual observations, including effects on attention/behavior.
13. [M] Add, revise, study further or retire techniques in the personal Grimoire.
14. [Q] Compare two or more traditions: shared concepts, differences, context and evidenced historical influence.
15. [Q] Separate personal experience, symbolism, testable claims and metaphysical interpretation.
16. [Q] Examine evidence/alternatives and update confidence or relationship to a belief.
17. [Y] Review beliefs, commitments, practice effects and a revisable personal statement of worldview.
18. [Y] Choose future study without requiring a permanent final doctrine.
19. [Reference] Study areas: astrology's traditions, astronomy/observation, tarot/divination symbolism, mysticism, prayer/ceremony, folklore, manifestation, dreams, mythology, philosophy, theology and comparative religion. Present them as study options, not equivalent scientific claims.
20. [Reference] Practice record: name, origin, purpose, method, date/context, expected/observed outcome, interpretations/alternatives and next decision.
21. [Reference] Comparative questions: reality, consciousness, central human problem, practices, promised outcomes, shared/distinctive concepts, historical/testable claims, experiential/metaphysical claims, and possible place in the user's worldview.
22. [Reference] Development lenses: observation, knowledge, practice, discernment and integration; no invented spiritual-power measurement.

## C17 — Mental well-being, rest and recreation → Renewal / Vitality

1. [D] Protect time without practical obligations and access to enjoyable activities.
2. [D/W] Notice self-reported stress, mood, energy and need for rest; no diagnosis from app use.
3. [W] Participate in hobbies/entertainment without a mandatory productivity objective.
4. [W] Spend time in preferred environments, including outdoors where desired/possible.
5. [W/M] Review stressors and whether routines allow adequate recovery.
6. [M/Q] Adjust excessive obligations or unsatisfying habits.
7. [C/P] Seek appropriate support when difficulties warrant it; do not schedule urgent needs for a distant annual review.
8. [Q] Review enjoyment versus obligation without requiring every leisure activity to be tracked.

## C18 — Learning, creativity and personal skills → Craft

1. [W] Allocate chosen study/practice time and complete a meaningful session.
2. [W] Save useful notes, methods and references.
3. [W] Advance a selected creative/personal project.
4. [M] Review learning progress and subjects to pursue, pause or discontinue.
5. [M] Review unfinished work and study examples from experienced practitioners.
6. [Q] Complete/share/publish a milestone where appropriate; private work remains valid.
7. [Q] Consolidate knowledge into usable workflows and experiment with a technique.
8. [Q] Review balance among learning, practicing and producing.
9. [Y] Review skills developed and choose future study.
10. [Y] Archive finished/abandoned projects and reassess interests.

## C19 — Safety, emergency readiness and contingencies → cross-discipline Wards

1. [M/Q] Inspect first-aid supplies and replenish relevant consumables.
2. [M/Q/P] Review emergency water, food and essential medical/household supplies using appropriate local/official guidance.
3. [M/Q] Check flashlights, batteries, charging and emergency power equipment.
4. [M/Q] Review household/vehicle emergency kits and labeled expirations/condition.
5. [M/Q/E] Maintain emergency contacts and accessible copies of essential documents.
6. [S/Y] Review evacuation routes, meeting locations and communications.
7. [S/Y/E] Prepare for relevant regional hazards and power outages without requiring precise location collection.
8. [Y/P] Understand safe utility-shutoff procedures and responsible-party contacts.
9. [M/Q] Review locks, essential security and relevant exterior lighting.
10. [E] Update plans when household, mobility, health, pet or dependent needs change.

## C20 — Seasonal, outdoor, travel and optional responsibilities → applicable disciplines

1. [S] Transition seasonal clothing/footwear; link to Regalia rather than duplicate actions.
2. [S] Inspect/service recreation, sports, exercise and outdoor equipment before use/storage.
3. [S] Prepare relevant heating/cooling and weather-related vehicle needs; link existing practices.
4. [S/P] Maintain yard, garden, landscaping, drainage and outdoor areas only where responsible.
5. [S] Inspect/clean seasonal storage.
6. [D/P] Pet feeding, grooming, supplies and veterinary/preventive care where applicable.
7. [P/C] Plant watering, pests, roots/soil and garden-equipment care where applicable.
8. [E] Review travel documents, luggage, chargers, insurance and preparation for an actual trip.
9. [P/C] Maintain bicycles, instruments, cameras, camping gear and specialist hobby tools.
10. [E/P] Maintain dependent-care logistics only when the user actually holds that responsibility.

## C21 — Life direction and personal systems → Renewal / Stewardship

1. [M] Review current priorities and accumulated unfinished responsibilities.
2. [M] Remove obsolete/redundant tasks and simplify the maintenance system.
3. [M/Q] Review time allocation relative to chosen objectives.
4. [Q] Review financial, social, creative, personal and learning progress without ranking human worth.
5. [Q] Reallocate attention toward desired neglected areas.
6. [Q] Revise goals that no longer fit and consider useful changes to living arrangements/routines.
7. [Y] Inventory important possessions, commitments and recurring responsibilities.
8. [Y] Update major goals and identify foreseeable transitions.
9. [Y] Review which activities/possessions still serve a purpose and form the next period's plan.
10. [Reference] Review rituals: daily foundation; weekly reset; monthly inspection; quarterly audit; semiannual transition; annual review. Link to underlying records instead of duplicating completion credit.
11. [Reference] Preserve the resource distinction: consumables, wear items, durable assets, living systems/obligations. Use replenishment, inspection/service or attention/review as appropriate.
12. [Reference] Track only useful metadata: category, action/item, function, cadence, last actual completion, next review, trigger, importance, cost/effort, responsible person and notes. Do not require every field for every quest.
