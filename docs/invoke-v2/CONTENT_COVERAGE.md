## Provenance and review boundaries

The preserved source is `CODEX_SOURCE.md`, extracted from the user-provided Invoke 2.0 brief. These are organizing suggestions. They have not been presented as reviewed clinical, financial, legal, safety or model-specific standards. The bundled records supply no fabricated citations or universal replacement ages. Exact medication, screening, product, vehicle, legal and expiry schedules require the user’s actual instructions or dates. All actionable templates require configuration/review before activation. Health and safety entries retain referral and responsible-party boundaries; the app supplies no hazardous procedures or treatment changes.

Clothing/footwear/device care records distinguish inspection, replenishment, condition-based repair/replacement and actual labeled expiry. Bonds uses chosen contact preferences; Arcana separates sources, observation and interpretation. The complete original wording and source IDs remain searchable in every template.
